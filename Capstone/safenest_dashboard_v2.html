Research Contributions
Context-Weighted Fusion (CWF v3)
Logistic-regression fusion of 6 detection signals × 10-D Bangladesh-aware context vector. Encodes prayer times, load-shedding hours, and local ISP trust. F1 = 0.983 · AUC = 0.9994 · ECE = 0.0079
TRAV 2.0 — Trusted Remote Access Verification
5-component behavioral trust engine. Solves: owner from abroad vs attacker from same country. Owner trust 0.92 · Attacker trust 0.28 · Separation 0.64
Three-Component Zero-Day Ensemble
Isolation Forest + Denoising Autoencoder + GRU Temporal. Trained on benign traffic only — zero attack label exposure. AUC = 0.969
